Purchase approval

A purchase order needs the approval of the manager. A skail function asks the manager and waits for the decision until a deadline; with no answer it goes to the director and waits again; with no answer again, it cancels the order. The wait is durable: it holds no thread or process, and it goes on even with the app closed.

You play the manager and the director:

  1. Click Place purchase order.
  2. Approve or reject it, or do nothing and watch the deadline (1 minute per level) move it along.
